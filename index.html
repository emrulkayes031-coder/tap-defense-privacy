1. Who this policy covers

WonderBox Lab provides Tap Defense for Android (com.zai.tapdefense). This policy covers the Android app, whose stated target audience includes ages 9–12 and 13–15. Tap Defense does not require a game account. The game itself can load and save progress offline; advertising, consent messages, and Google Play purchases use an internet connection when available.

Offline playGame progress and settings are stored on the device.
Google servicesAdMob, User Messaging Platform, and Play Billing are integrated.
No game sign-inPurchases still use the user's Google Play account.

2. Information handled by the app and SDKs

The Android source stores gameplay data in WebView local storage and app preferences. It does not show a developer-operated account service or analytics SDK. Integrated Google SDKs can process data when their features run, even though gameplay works offline.

InformationPurpose and handling
Game progress and settingsLevels, stars, gems, characters, upgrades, and sound/vibration choices are saved locally so play can continue. The app also keeps a rewarded-ad claim count and interstitial timing in app preferences. Android backup and device transfer may include this local data when enabled.
Ad and device dataGoogle says its Mobile Ads SDK can automatically collect and share IP address (which can indicate approximate location), app and ad interactions such as launches, taps and video views, diagnostics, and device or account identifiers for advertising, measurement, and fraud prevention. The Android manifest removes advertising-ID and certain Privacy Sandbox ad permissions; this does not rule out other identifiers handled by Google.
Consent choicesGoogle's User Messaging Platform (UMP) checks whether a privacy message or options form is needed and manages applicable ad privacy choices. Choices and related technical information may be processed by Google to apply those preferences.
Purchase informationGoogle Play Billing provides product, purchase-status, token, and signed purchase details to the app to validate an optional purchase and restore access. The app stores signed purchase evidence locally; its backup rules exclude that purchase cache. Google Play processes the transaction and payment information under Google's policies.

Tap Defense does not ask you to enter a name, email address, or payment-card number in the game. If you email WonderBox Lab, your message and email address will be used to respond to your request.

3. Advertising and sharing with Google

Tap Defense integrates Google AdMob for interstitial ads after eligible completed levels and optional rewarded ads that can grant in-game gems. Ad requests are configured for child-directed treatment, a G maximum content rating, and non-personalized ads for all users. The app's manifest removes the Android advertising-ID permission. These settings do not mean that no information reaches Google: the Mobile Ads SDK may still send the data described above to Google to deliver and measure ads and protect against fraud.

Google's Mobile Ads SDK data disclosure, Privacy Policy, and information about partner apps provide more detail. Ad content may be supplied through Google's ad systems and applicable advertising partners.

4. Consent and ad privacy choices

The app uses Google UMP to check for applicable privacy messages. When a message is required and available, it can present choices before ads are requested. An Ad Privacy Options control appears in the game's Shop when UMP reports that it is required; it can reopen Google's options form while the device is online. If ad requests are not permitted, the app does not load ads. Your choices do not prevent offline play.

Available choices depend on your region and the messages configured in WonderBox Lab' AdMob account. For an explanation of UMP, see Google's UMP guidance.

5. In-app purchases

The Android project contains an optional one-time Google Play purchase to unlock game content and remove forced interstitial ads. Optional rewarded ads may remain available. Google Play handles checkout and payment; Tap Defense uses purchase status and signed purchase details to verify, save, and restore the entitlement. Tap Defense does not receive or store a payment-card number in its game code.

Purchases and refunds are subject to Google's Privacy Policy and the Google Payments Privacy Notice.

6. Children's privacy

Tap Defense targets ages 9–12 and 13–15. The reviewed Android code applies child-directed ad treatment, a G ad-content rating, non-personalized ad requests, and UMP's under-age-of-consent setting to all users. It does not request a birth date or game account. Google's SDK data handling described above can still apply. Parents or guardians may contact WonderBox Lab about a child's information using the contact address below.

7. Security, retention, and deletion

Game data and verified purchase evidence are saved in Android app storage. The app disallows cleartext network traffic in its Android configuration, and Google states that Mobile Ads SDK data is encrypted in transit. No system can guarantee absolute security.

Local gameplay data and app preferences remain until they are cleared, overwritten, or removed with the app, subject to Android backup and restore settings. Verified purchase evidence is excluded from the app's backup rules and can be removed when an online Google Play ownership check no longer finds the purchase. Google may retain ad, consent, and transaction information according to its own policies. WonderBox Lab does not operate a separate game account database in the reviewed project.

8. Your choices and rights

  • You can play offline. Ads and purchases need a connection.
  • Where available, use Shop → Ad Privacy Options to review applicable UMP choices.
  • You can clear Tap Defense's app data or uninstall the app to remove the on-device save. Android backups may remain under your device or Google backup settings; clearing data may also erase progress.
  • For access, deletion, or other privacy requests concerning information controlled by WonderBox Lab, contact the address below. The handling of a request depends on the information available and applicable law. For Google-held information, use Google Account privacy controls and Google's privacy resources.
